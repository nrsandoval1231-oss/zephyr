## Audit notes on the reel itself

Things a recruiting coordinator would flag, as the package stands:

1. **Pre-snap is eating the tape.** Play 2 is 16.9 seconds for one snap; Play 9
   is 19.1 seconds; Play 10 is 16.9. Roughly **half the reel is players standing
   still.** For a first-look coach reel, trimming to ~2 seconds of pre-snap would
   fit four or five more plays into the same 2:21.
2. **Only one play is labeled.** Play 9 carries a "29-YARD TOUCHDOWN PASS" card
   and it's by far the easiest play to evaluate. Every other clip is just
   "OPPONENT | PASS." Down, distance, and result on each clip would do more for
   this reel than any other single edit — and the stadium scoreboard is already
   readable on the end-zone clips, so the data is sitting right there.
3. **The two best plays are buried.** The end-zone-to-end-zone throw (Play 10) is
   at 1:51 and the off-platform throw with a free rusher (Play 4) is at 0:41.
   Coaches quit early. Those belong in the first 30 seconds.
4. **`..._Description.txt` still has two open TODOs**: `H/W: Not provided` and
   `Full games: Add a no-login Hudl or unlisted YouTube link before publishing`.
   Height/weight is table stakes for a QB evaluation.
5. **The chapter markers in the description are accurate** — 0:06 pocket/timing,
   1:08 downfield placement, 2:09 red zone all line up with what's on screen.
6. **No arm-strength throw is isolated.** Plays 8 and 10 are the deep shots, and
   both are shot from far enough away that the ball is hard to track. A tight
   sideline angle on one vertical would sell the arm better than either.
